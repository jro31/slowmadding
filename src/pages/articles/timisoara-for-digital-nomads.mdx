## Vegan-friendly restaurants in Timișoara

I mostly cooked in Timișoara so didn't get around to too many restaurants here, but it's a pretty easy place to be vegan if you do want to eat out.

One thing I learned while here, which is slightly concerning seeing as this is my third time in Romania, as that 'de post' essentially means vegan - it means that food contains no meat, dairy or eggs. So look out for eateries that advertise 'de post'. Once you know that, you realise that a lot of places are vegan friendly.

By far my favourite restaurant of the places that I did go was <ExternalLink url='https://maps.app.goo.gl/tTc6kq8hDRnnwyYZ7'>Biofresh</ExternalLink> in the city centre. It's listed as a vegetarian restaurant, but everything I asked about on the menu was confirmed vegan. It's freshly-cooked, healthy food.

Another place that I went to multiple times was <ExternalLink url='https://maps.app.goo.gl/1qX1X1jYXtATz6237'>Falafel King</ExternalLink> in the Iulius Town food court. It's a falafel-based restaurant if you couldn't tell, and they have an entire 'de post' menu. The food is decent - it's vegan at least, but the people making it do like to be a bit over-enthusiastic with the sauce, so temper your expectations.

## Getting vegan groceries in Timișoara

All roughly equal distance from my room were a branch of <ExternalLink url='https://maps.app.goo.gl/FJkTkpdgLwDatLMw6'>Lidl</ExternalLink>, a smallish <ExternalLink url='https://maps.app.goo.gl/fui6dDC5rFXoAQhv9'>Carrefour</ExternalLink>, and a big <ExternalLink url='https://maps.app.goo.gl/pNmhMwh6d2TGtteF6'>Mega Image</ExternalLink>. Between the three of them I was able to get everything I needed in Timișoara.

Lidl was the best for fruit and for mixed nuts, but not much else. Carrefour had the best quality and best value soy milk, the biggest range of tofu and of rice, and was the best place for more specific nuts and seeds. Everything else I needed I got at Mega Image, which is always very vegan-friendly.

Vegan grocery shopping in Timișoara is pretty easy.

## Getting vegan supplements in Timișoara

Coming here from Bucharest where I'd stocked-up, I didn't have to buy too many supplmenents in Timișoara.

The one thing that I did need was vegan protein powder, and the best that I found was in <ExternalLink url='https://maps.app.goo.gl/pj5f9zfvqX9m2Py49'>Vitabolic</ExternalLink>, which is a local brand with corresponding prices. 750g of really good vegan protein powder only cost 80 lei. They had a tonne of other stuff on their shelves, so worth coming here for a look. The staff speak English.

The only other supplement I bought was a opportune purchase of creatine in <ExternalLink url='https://maps.app.goo.gl/WoiPs78tjUSQajM57'>Decathlon</ExternalLink>. They also sell vegan protein powder, but it's almost twice the price of that at Vitabolic, costing 150 lei for 750g.

Very frustatingly I stumbled across a shop called Enigma Plant in <ExternalLink url='https://maps.app.goo.gl/yum8G8bguaPNchQY7'>Iulius Town</ExternalLink> opposite Primark, on my final day in Timișoara. I had a quick look round and it looked like a shop where, had I not been leaving the next morning, would have had some useful things.

The name is misleading as it's not all plant supplements - they sell whey protein, for example, but it seemed to be mainly plant-based supplements and health foods.
